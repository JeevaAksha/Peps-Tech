Andhra Pradesh

Capital: Amaravati. Known for agriculture and a long coastline.

Major language: Telugu.

Arunachal Pradesh

Capital: Itanagar. India's easternmost state.

Known for mountains, forests, and tribal cultures.

Assam

Capital: Dispur. Famous for tea plantations.

Home to Kaziranga National Park.

Bihar

Capital: Patna. One of India's oldest inhabited regions.

Known for Nalanda and rich history.

Chhattisgarh

Capital: Raipur. Rich in minerals and forests.

Known for steel and power industries.

Goa

Capital: Panaji. India's smallest state by area.

Famous for beaches and tourism.

Gujarat

Capital: Gandhinagar. Birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi.

Known for business and the Gir Forest.

Haryana

Capital: Chandigarh. Important agricultural state.

Contributes significantly to India's economy.

Himachal Pradesh

Capital: Shimla. Located in the Himalayas.

Popular for tourism and apple farming.

Jharkhand

Capital: Ranchi. Rich in mineral resources.

Known for forests and waterfalls.

Karnataka

Capital: Bengaluru. India's IT hub.

Known for technology and heritage sites.

Kerala

Capital: Thiruvananthapuram. Famous for backwaters.

Known for high literacy and tourism.

Madhya Pradesh

Capital: Bhopal. Called the Heart of India.

Known for wildlife sanctuaries and temples.

Maharashtra

Capital: Mumbai. Financial capital of India.

Home to Bollywood and major industries.

Manipur

Capital: Imphal. Located in Northeast India.

Known for Loktak Lake and rich culture.

Meghalaya

Capital: Shillong. Known as the Abode of Clouds.

Famous for living root bridges.

Mizoram

Capital: Aizawl. Predominantly hilly state.

Known for scenic landscapes.

Nagaland

Capital: Kohima. Home to many tribal communities.

Known for the Hornbill Festival.

Odisha

Capital: Bhubaneswar. Famous for temples.

Home to the Jagannath Temple.

Punjab

Capital: Chandigarh. Known as India's breadbasket.

Famous for agriculture and Sikh heritage.

Rajasthan

Capital: Jaipur. India's largest state by area.

Known for deserts, forts, and palaces.

Sikkim

Capital: Gangtok. India's least populous state.

Known for mountains and biodiversity.

Tamil Nadu

Capital: Chennai. Known for temples and classical culture.

Major industrial and educational hub.

Telangana

Capital: Hyderabad. Major technology center.

Known for heritage monuments and IT parks.

Tripura

Capital: Agartala. Northeastern state with rich culture.

Known for bamboo and handicrafts.

Uttar Pradesh

Capital: Lucknow. India's most populous state.

Home to the Taj Mahal in Agra.

Uttarakhand

Capital: Dehradun. Known for the Himalayas.

Popular for pilgrimage and adventure tourism.

West Bengal

Capital: Kolkata. Known for literature and culture.

Home to the Sundarbans mangrove forest.
